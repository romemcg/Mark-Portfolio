The problem

Event teams run invitations, guest lists, check in and follow up across spreadsheets, chat threads and paper lists. Guests feel it as queues at the door, and organisers are left with little data once the day ends.

My role

Product lead. I shaped the studio offer and service lines, designed the studio website and the event platform, and produced the product walkthrough.

Product decisions

  • One guest journey, not separate tools. Invitation, registration, check in and follow up share one guest record, so organisers see the whole event in one place.
  • Mobile first for guests. Registration takes under a minute and the ticket lives on the phone.
  • Delivered as a service. The agency runs the platform for each event, so clients get the experience without adopting new software.
  • Design the full experience first. A complete demo and walkthrough define every step of the guest journey before development starts.

1. Overview

A creative technology studio inside an events and creative agency. Its first product is one connected platform for conferences and corporate events that covers the guest journey from invitation to follow up, and the organiser's view of the event as it happens. The agency runs the platform for each client as a service.

2. Goals and non goals

Goals

  • Replace spreadsheets, chat threads and paper lists with one guest record.
  • Let guests register in under a minute and carry their ticket on their phone.
  • Remove queues at the door with QR check in.
  • Give organisers real time data during the event and a follow up afterwards.

Non goals

  • No standalone software for clients to adopt. The agency operates it.
  • No development before the full guest journey is designed and demonstrated.

3. Users and needs

Event organiser

One place for invitations, guest lists, check in and results.

Guest

A fast, clear journey from invitation to entry and follow up.

Business association or corporate team

A branded event experience without new software to learn.

5. Functional requirements

P0 is required for the first release. P1 follows. P2 needs evidence first.

A1P0

Branded invitation

An invitation with one tap RSVP.

Acceptance: A guest responds from the invitation without creating an account.

A2P0

Mobile registration

Mobile first registration.

Acceptance: Registration takes under a minute.

A3P0

QR ticket

An instant QR ticket, calendar invite and reminders.

Acceptance: The ticket lives on the phone and is ready immediately after registration.

A4P0

QR check in

Scan at the entrance and print a badge.

Acceptance: A guest is checked in and holds a badge in seconds.

A5P1

Live program

A live program with Q&A and polls.

Acceptance: Guests can ask questions and vote during sessions.

A6P1

Organiser dashboard

Registrations, check ins and session ratings in real time.

Acceptance: The organiser sees the state of the event without asking anyone.

A7P1

Post event follow up

Follow up messages and results from the shared guest record.

Acceptance: Follow up uses the same record as registration and check in.

6. Non functional requirements

Mobile first
Guest screens are designed for phones first, with tablet check in at the door.
Brand
Each event can carry its own branding across invitation, ticket and badge.
Delivery model
Operated by the agency per event, so clients need no onboarding.

7. Scope

In scope

  • The guest journey from invitation to follow up
  • Organiser dashboard
  • A studio website and a two minute narrated walkthrough

Out of scope

  • Self service software for clients
  • Full development before a first live event

10. Risks and mitigations

Events vary widely, so a single flow may not fit every client.

Mitigation: Run the first event closely and adjust the flow.

Live event conditions such as poor connectivity at the door.

Mitigation: Plan an offline friendly check in and a manual fallback.

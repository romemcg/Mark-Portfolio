A mobile alarm and morning focus app for heavy sleepers, built around wake challenges instead of a snooze button.

The problem

My role

Product decisions

  • Challenge over snooze. The design removes the easy way back to sleep.

1. Overview

A mobile alarm and morning focus app for heavy sleepers. Instead of a snooze button, the alarm keeps ringing until the user completes a sequence of wake challenges: walking, a camera check at a chosen wake location and a short movement task, followed by a focus reminder. The prototype is built with managed Expo and structured so that native alarm modules can replace the managed parts later.

2. Goals and non goals

Goals

  • Make it hard to dismiss an alarm and fall back asleep.
  • Confirm that the user is physically up, moving and awake.
  • Protect the first minutes of the morning with a focus step.
  • Be honest about what the platform can and cannot guarantee.

Non goals

  • No claim of overriding silent mode or Do Not Disturb in the managed prototype.
  • No real blocking of other apps. Focus mode is a reminder only.
  • No face recognition, GPS verification or machine learning scene matching.

3. Users and needs

Heavy sleeper

An alarm that cannot be dismissed half asleep, with an exit if something goes wrong.

4. User stories

  • As a heavy sleeper, I want the alarm to continue until I complete a challenge, so that I cannot go back to sleep.
  • As a user, I want to preview and choose my alarm sound, so that waking up is not unpleasant.
  • As a user, I want an assisted exit, so that I am never trapped by my own alarm.

5. Functional requirements

P0 is required for the first release. P1 follows. P2 needs evidence first.

W1P0

Alarm management

Create, edit, enable, disable and delete alarms.

Acceptance: Alarms persist on the device.

W2P0

Notification delivery

Local notifications, with a maximum importance channel on Android.

Acceptance: A scheduled alarm raises a notification with sound and vibration while the app is running.

W3P0

Looping alarm audio

Looping sound with preview, sound selection, a volume ramp over about 15 seconds and haptics.

Acceptance: Audio continues through every challenge screen and stops only on success or an assisted exit.

W4P0

Wake challenge flow

Walking, then a camera check, then a 20 second movement challenge, then focus.

Acceptance: The alarm stops only when the sequence is complete.

W5P0

Wake location check

A reference photo of the wake location compared with a live photo.

Acceptance: The check is a labelled image fingerprint heuristic and is described that way in the interface.

W6P1

Morning focus reminder

A reminder after waking to start the first task.

Acceptance: Clearly labelled as a reminder, not app blocking.

W7P1

Permission handling

Handles granted, denied, restricted and unavailable states.

Acceptance: Each state has a clear explanation and a safe fallback.

W8P1

Web simulation

A simulation mode for web with guarded native calls.

Acceptance: No sensor, camera or notification call is made on web.

W9P2

Native alarm modules

Swap the alarm scheduler and audio services for native implementations.

Acceptance: The interface does not change when the services are replaced.

6. Non functional requirements

Persistence
Alarms, settings, onboarding, history and the active alarm session are stored on the device.
Safety
An assisted exit always exists. Safety requirements are defined before features.
Architecture
Swappable service interfaces for scheduling, audio and app blocking.
Quality
TypeScript checks, linting and Jest tests.
Honesty
Interface copy never claims capabilities the platform does not provide.

7. Scope

In scope

  • Alarms, looping audio, challenge flow, wake location photo, focus reminder, local storage and web simulation

Out of scope

  • Guaranteed delivery after force quit
  • Silent mode and Do Not Disturb override
  • Cross app blocking
  • Cloud accounts

10. Risks and mitigations

Scheduled notifications may not fire after a force quit.

Mitigation: Move to native alarms. Document the limit until then.

Android battery savers delay notifications and background audio.

Mitigation: Native foreground service and user guidance.

The camera heuristic produces false results with lighting changes.

Mitigation: Label it honestly and keep an assisted exit.

Silent mode and Do Not Disturb can mute lock screen sounds.

Mitigation: Do not claim an override. Use native alarm audio sessions later.

11. Open questions

  • How many challenges are too many for a half awake user?
  • Which native platform to build first?

12. Known limitations

  • Managed Expo cannot guarantee alarm delivery after force quit.
  • Silent mode and Do Not Disturb are not overridden.
  • Camera verification is a rough heuristic.
  • Pedometer availability varies, so an accelerometer fallback is used.
  • Focus mode is a reminder only.
